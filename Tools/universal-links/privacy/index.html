AAGA Claims Privacy Policy

Accurate Adjustments GA · Effective October 20, 2026

AAGA Claims is the mobile app for the Accurate Adjustments GA client portal. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. The app shows the same claim and lead records as the portal, and it's available only to people with a portal account.

Information we handle

  • Account details: your name, email address and phone number from your portal account.
  • Claim and lead information: the claims and leads you're part of, including loss addresses, insurance details, updates, payments, tasks and messages.
  • Photos and files you upload: documents and photos you add to a claim or lead. Photos are sent without the location where they were taken.
  • Support requests: questions and replies you send to our team.
  • Notification token: if you allow notifications, a token that lets us send updates to your device.
  • Diagnostics: if the app crashes or stops responding, a technical report (app version, device model, iOS version and where the app failed). These reports don't contain what you typed or your claim details.

How we use it

  • To show and manage your claims and leads, and to communicate with you about them.
  • To send the notifications you've allowed, such as new updates and recorded payments.
  • To keep the app working and fix problems.

We don't sell your information, show you ads, or track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Where it's stored

Your information is stored in our Salesforce system, which also runs the client portal. Salesforce processes it for us under its own security and privacy commitments. On your device, the app keeps a short-lived copy of recent screens so they work offline; signing out clears it. Your sign-in is protected by Salesforce and stored in the iPhone's keychain.

Sharing

We share claim information only as needed to handle your claim, for example with your insurance company, contractors you've authorised, or as the law requires.

Your choices

  • Notifications: turn them on or off in the app (Account) or in iOS Settings.
  • Face ID lock: optional, in Account.
  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Account → Delete Account. We delete your account and personal information within 30 days, keeping only records the law requires for your claims.
  • Questions or requests: contact us at [privacy contact email].

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
